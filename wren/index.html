Questions

Good to know.

Something else on your mind? Call us on 020 7946 0321 and we’ll talk it through.

Are you taking on new patients?

Yes. Adults and children are welcome. Book a new patient check-up online or by phone, and we’ll send a short medical history form to fill in before you come.

Do you offer NHS treatment?

Wren is a private practice, so we don’t offer NHS treatment. Our fees are listed above, and your written plan confirms the full cost before treatment starts.

I’m nervous about the dentist. Can you help?

Tell us when you book. We’ll give you a longer, quieter first appointment, explain everything before we do it and agree a signal so you can pause at any time.

Are clear aligners right for me?

They suit many adults, but not everyone. We’ll examine your teeth and take a 3D scan first, then explain whether aligners or another option would work better, and what results are realistic.

Can I spread the cost of treatment?

Our Wren Care membership spreads the cost of routine care across the year. For larger treatment, ask us about paying in stages as your treatment progresses.
