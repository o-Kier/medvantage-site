Common questions

How much does a website cost?

It depends on what your practice needs: the number of pages and treatments, booking integration, copywriting and photography. After your free review we send a fixed quote, so you know the full cost before anything starts.

How long does a new website take?

Most sites take three to six weeks from our first call, depending on size and how quickly feedback comes back. We'll give you a date before we start.

Do I own my website?

Yes. The site, the content and the domain are yours, whether or not you stay on a care plan.

Can you work with my booking system?

Usually, yes. We connect the booking and practice management tools clinics already use, so patients can book in a couple of taps.

Do you write the content?

Yes. We write it with you, in plain English, and within the advertising rules for your profession.

What happens to my details?

We use them to prepare your review and reply to your enquiry. That's it. Read our privacy policy
