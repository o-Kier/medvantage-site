Finding 01 · InstagramIllustrative example
As posted

“Book your Botox with Sarah today #botox”

Consider instead

“Thinking about lines and wrinkles? Book a consultation with Sarah to talk through your options.”

Why: Botox is a prescription-only medicine. UK rules allow clinics to promote the consultation, not the medicine.

[LAW] Human Medicines Regulations 2012, reg 284 · [CAP RULE] CAP Code 12.12 · Suggested wording for qualified review
