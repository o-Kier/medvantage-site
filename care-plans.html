Common questions

Do I need a care plan?

A website needs hosting, security updates and backups to keep working. A care plan covers all of that for one monthly price, so nothing is left to chance.

Is there a minimum term?

Yes, six months. After that, your plan carries on month to month.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. You can move up or down a plan as your practice changes.

What if I need more changes than my plan covers?

Just ask. We'll tell you what it involves and agree a price before we start.

Do I own my website?

Yes. The site, the content and the domain are yours, whether or not you stay on a care plan.

Can you look after a site you didn't build?

Sometimes. Tell us about your current site with a free review request, and we'll let you know honestly.
