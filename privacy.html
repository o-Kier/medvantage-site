The short version

  • We collect only what we need to prepare Snapshots, work with clients and run a small business.
  • We never sell your details, and we never share a clinic's findings with anyone else.
  • Every marketing email we send lets you opt out, and once you do, we won't contact you again.
  • We review only what clinics have chosen to publish, and we keep evidence no longer than we need it.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing hello@medvantage.uk.

01Who we are

MedVantage is the trading name of Kieran Hymers, a sole trader based in London, England. In this policy, "MedVantage", "we" and "us" mean that business.

We are the "controller" of the personal information described here, which means we decide how and why it is used, and we are responsible for looking after it under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018) and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR).

Questions about your information? Email hello@medvantage.uk and we'll reply as soon as we can.

03Clinics' public marketing

Our Snapshots, audits and monthly Watch reports look at what clinics have chosen to publish: websites, social media posts, review profiles and similar. Those pages often include information about people, such as practitioners, reviewers and, in before-and-after images, patients.

We don't set out to collect information about individuals here. We look at this material only to comment on the clinic's marketing. To show a clinic exactly where a point appears, we may keep a screenshot or link as evidence. Where a screenshot includes an identifiable patient, we limit or obscure that part where we can, we never use it for any other purpose, and we delete it in line with section 07.

If you appear in a clinic's marketing and want to know whether we hold anything about you, or want it removed, email hello@medvantage.uk.

04Emails to clinics

We contact some clinics we haven't spoken to before, to offer a free Snapshot. We keep this careful and limited:

  • We send unsolicited emails only to limited companies and LLPs, and only to people whose role makes our services relevant to them, such as owners and managers.
  • We don't send unsolicited marketing emails to sole traders or partnerships. We contact them only if they've asked us to, or through someone they already know.
  • Every email says who we are and gives you a simple way to opt out. Just reply and say so.
  • We send each email individually. We don't use tracking pixels or track whether you open our emails.
  • If you opt out, we keep a brief record (your name, email and clinic) on a do-not-contact list so that we never contact you again.

05Who we share it with

We don't sell personal information, and we never publish or share a clinic's findings with anyone other than that clinic.

We use trusted service providers who handle information on our behalf and only on our instructions:

  • Google: email, document storage and spreadsheets (Google Workspace), and website fonts
  • FormSubmit: delivers website form submissions to our inbox
  • GitHub: hosts this website
  • Cloudflare: website security, performance and visitor statistics
  • Notion: planning and working notes
  • Anthropic: AI tools that help analyse public marketing and draft reports
  • Our bank, any payment provider we use, and our accountant, for client billing and records

We may also share information with professional advisers such as lawyers or insurers, or with authorities where the law requires it.

06Information outside the UK

Some of our service providers are based outside the UK, including in the United States, so your information may be processed there. When it is, we rely on protections recognised under UK law, such as the UK–US data bridge or the ICO's international data transfer agreement or addendum, so that your information keeps an equivalent level of protection.

08This website and cookies

This website doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. Our hosting and security providers (GitHub and Cloudflare) receive technical information when you visit, as any website does, and Cloudflare may set a small, strictly necessary cookie to help tell people apart from automated traffic.

We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which doesn't use cookies or build a profile of you. The fonts on this site are loaded from Google, which means Google receives your IP address when the page loads.

09Keeping it safe

We keep personal information in a small number of secure, password-protected business accounts with two-step sign-in, and we limit what we collect in the first place. No system is perfectly secure, but if something did go wrong we would act quickly and tell you and the ICO where the law requires.

10Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • ask us to correct anything that's inaccurate or incomplete
  • ask us to delete your information
  • ask us to restrict how we use it
  • object to our using it on the basis of legitimate interests, and to stop marketing at any time; if you object to marketing, we'll always stop
  • ask for information you gave us in a portable format, where that applies

To use any of these rights, email hello@medvantage.uk. It's free, and we'll respond within one month. We may need to confirm who you are first.

11Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first at hello@medvantage.uk so we can try to put it right.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, the UK's data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

12Changes to this policy

We'll update this policy when the way we work changes, including if MedVantage becomes a limited company. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
