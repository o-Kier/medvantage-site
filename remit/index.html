Questions

What consultants ask us.

Do I need to change my software or secretary?

No. We work with your existing Healthcode account and practice system. Many clients keep their secretary for bookings and letters, and hand us the billing.

Will my patients know you're involved?

Yes, and that helps. Patients get clear bills from a named person on your behalf, with a phone number and a card payment link, so they're never surprised by an unexplained call.

What happens when an insurer pays less than I invoiced?

We check whether it's an error or the agreed fee. Errors we challenge. Where your fee arrangements allow a shortfall, we invoice the patient clearly and politely.

Can you take on my old unpaid invoices?

Yes. We review your aged debt first, tell you what's realistically recoverable, and agree a rate before we start.

How do I leave if it isn't working?

Give us 30 days' notice. We hand back a full record of every invoice, payment and open query.
