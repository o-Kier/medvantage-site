A crown, day by day.

Every case is tracked from the moment it arrives, and you get a message when it’s on its way back.

  1. Day 0

    Scan in by 2pm

    Upload from your scanner or book a collection. Add the shade, photos and any notes.

  2. Day 1

    Design check

    Your technician reviews the margins and contacts, and calls you if anything needs a decision.

  3. Days 2–4

    Milled, fired, finished

    Milled in-house, then stained, glazed and checked on the model by hand.

  4. Day 5

    Back at your practice

    Tracked delivery with the statement of manufacture in the box.
