Our approach

We’d rather do less, and do it well.

Good skin rarely comes from one appointment. It comes from understanding what is going on underneath: your skin type, your medical history, your routine and what you have already tried.

From there, we choose the few things most likely to help. Sometimes that is a course of treatment. Sometimes it is a change to what is in your bathroom cabinet. We will always tell you which, and why.

“A good consultation should leave you clearer about your skin, whether or not you book anything.”

Dr Elena Marsh, Medical Director

Your visit

What happens when you come in.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    Forty-five minutes with a doctor or nurse prescriber. We look closely at your skin, go through your medical history and ask what you would like to change.

  2. 02

    Your written plan

    Your options, what each involves, what is realistic to expect and what it will cost. There is no obligation, and no pressure to decide on the day.

  3. 03

    Time to think

    For medical aesthetic treatments, we always leave time between your consultation and treatment, so any decision is one you have had space to make.

  4. 04

    Treatment and aftercare

    Treatment is carried out by a registered clinician in a private room. A follow-up review is included, and you can contact the clinic directly with any questions afterwards.

Our standards

Promises you can check.

Aesthetic treatment in the UK is still lightly regulated. These are the standards we hold ourselves to, written down so you can hold us to them too.

  • Consultation first, always

    No treatment without an assessment by a registered clinician. A consultation may not end in treatment, and that is a perfectly good outcome.

  • Named, registered clinicians

    You will always know who is treating you and how to check their registration.

  • Honest about results

    Everyone’s skin responds differently. We talk about what is realistic for you, including the possible side effects, before you decide.

  • No offers, no countdowns

    We don’t run discounts or limited-time deals on treatment. Decisions about your skin shouldn’t be rushed.

  • Over-18s only

    We only treat adults aged 18 and over, and may ask for ID at your first visit.

  • Clear prices

    Consultation fees and treatment prices are given in writing before you book, including any deposit.

Questions

Good questions to ask.

Something not covered here? Call us on 020 7946 0958 and a member of the clinical team will call you back.

Do I need a consultation before treatment?

Yes. Every patient has a consultation with a doctor or nurse prescriber before any treatment, even if you have had the same treatment elsewhere. It is how we decide, together, whether a treatment is suitable for you.

What does the consultation cost?

A skin consultation is £75 and lasts 45 minutes. You leave with a written plan whether or not you choose to go ahead with treatment.

Will I need time off afterwards?

It depends on the treatment and on your skin. Some people have redness or sensitivity for a day or two, sometimes longer. We will talk through what to expect for you specifically, and plan around anything important in your diary.

Do you offer treatments for lines and wrinkles?

Our medical team offers consultations for lines, wrinkles and facial ageing. We will discuss all the options, including ones that don’t involve treatment at all. A consultation may not result in treatment, and we always leave time between consultation and any treatment.

What if my skin condition needs medical care?

If we see something that should be looked at by your GP or a dermatologist, we will tell you and write to them with your consent. We won’t offer cosmetic treatment in place of the right medical care.

Can I bring someone with me?

Of course. Many people like a second pair of ears for the consultation. Just let us know when you book.
